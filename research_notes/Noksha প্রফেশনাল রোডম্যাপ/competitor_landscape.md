# Competitor landscape for Noksha (landing / funnel / offer builders)

Geographic lens: Bangladesh Facebook-ads + COD sellers; WordPress plugin vs global SaaS. Sources prioritized: official product/docs pages and WordPress.org; secondary reviews used only where they restate concrete plan/feature facts. Note on DigiShop: the publicly documented product matching that name in 2026 search results is **DigiShop AI** (SaaS storefront), not a WordPress.org COD plugin; BD-local WordPress competitors documented below include Bangla Landing Page, Eazifiy, COD Popup Checkout, StackNudge Store Kit, Tamai Funnel, and BD payment gateways.

## What do CartFlows, FunnelKit, ClickFunnels, Systeme.io, GetResponse/GetCourse-style builders, Thrive Architect, Elementor + Woo, and Bangladesh-focused COD tools do that sellers expect as table stakes?

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026, “professional” means a guided path from ad click → offer page → short order form → payment/COD → thank-you/upsell, plus pixels, step analytics, and iteration (templates + A/B or recovery)—not a pretty page alone. Global Woo plugins set the checkout/upsell bar; BD tools set the COD, Bangla, fake-order, and Dhaka shipping bar.

### Cited Findings
- CartFlows positions itself as replacing default WooCommerce checkout with conversion funnels: order bumps, one-click upsells, A/B testing, analytics, and ready-made templates; any WooCommerce payment gateway works, and COD is listed among gateways supported for one-click upsell. — [CartFlows funnel builder](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/); [CartFlows pricing FAQ](https://cartflows.com/pricing/)
- CartFlows Free vs paid comparison lists modern/multistep checkout, Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics pixel, checkout field editor, order bumps, one-click upsells, smart routing, A/B testing, and analytics as the capability ladder. — [CartFlows Free vs Pro](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/free-vs-pro/)
- FunnelKit (formerly WooFunnels) on WordPress.org and docs: unlimited funnels for optimized checkout, sales/landing pages, order bumps (Pro), rule-based one-click upsells (Pro), thank-you pages, lead/opt-in pages; templates are mobile-responsive and editable with popular page builders. — [FunnelKit WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/funnel-builder/); [FunnelKit Lite vs Pro](https://funnelkit.com/lite-vs-pro/)
- FunnelKit is packaged as a product suite (Funnel Builder, Automations, Cart/side cart, subscriptions) with separate free + Pro licenses—not a single monolith. — [FunnelKit FAQ](https://funnelkit.com/frequently-asked-questions/)
- ClickFunnels (SaaS) bundles drag-and-drop funnels, Smart Checkout, A/B testing, landing pages, store, courses/community, CRM, email automation, analytics, and Payments AI (Stripe/PayPal/NMI/wallets, gateway routing, failed-payment retries); plans from Launch $97/mo to Optimize $297/mo (Dominate $5,997/yr). — [ClickFunnels pricing docs](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/clickfunnels-pricing/); [Payments setup](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/getting-started-setting-up-payments-in-clickfunnels/); [Learning Revolution 2026 review](https://www.learningrevolution.net/clickfunnels-review/)
- Systeme.io includes funnel builder with drag-and-drop, templates, A/B tests, one-click upsells, order bumps, countdown/deadline funnels, funnel stats, Stripe/PayPal payments with 0% platform transaction fee; free plan includes funnels (limits differ across pages: features page vs pricing/sales pages). — [systeme.io features](https://systeme.io/features); [sales funnels](https://systeme.io/sales-funnels/); [pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing)
- GetResponse Conversion Funnel combines landing pages, forms, exit-intent popups, email nurture, sales/order pages, abandoned-order emails, one-click upsells, Facebook/Google ads inside the app, and processors (Stripe, PayPal, Square, BlueSnap, PayU). — [GetResponse conversion funnel](https://www.getresponse.com/features/conversion-funnel); [blog overview](https://www.getresponse.com/blog/getresponse-conversion-funnel)
- GetCourse-style all-in-one: page builder with forms (registration, mailing signup, payments), device/user-group visibility, auto-funnels tying landings + mailings + products + CRM; plans differ by active users but claim full feature set on each plan; Start from ~$65/mo or $467/yr. — [GetCourse website builder help](https://getcourse.io/blog/760039); [pricing help](https://getcourse.io/blog/776346); [help FAQ](https://getcourse.io/help); [auto-funnels](https://getcourse.io/processes)
- Thrive Architect: conversion-focused WP landing pages that bypass theme layout; 300+/357+ templates; responsive editing; A/B testing via Thrive Optimize add-on/bundle. — [Thrive Architect product](https://thrivethemes.com/thrive-architect-page-builder/); [landing pages docs](https://thrivethemes.com/docs/how-to-get-started-with-landing-pages-in-thrive-architect/); [template gallery](https://thrivethemes.com/landing-pages-gallery/)
- Elementor Pro WooCommerce Checkout widget: visual one/two-column checkout styling (labels, typography, sticky column, Place Order button) but does not replace checkout structure (field order, multistep, bumps)—funnel builders still needed for that layer. — [Elementor checkout blog](https://elementor.com/blog/customize-woocommerce-checkout-page/); [FunnelKit on Elementor checkout](https://funnelkit.com/woocommerce-checkout-elementor/); [Elementor ecommerce builder](https://elementor.com/ecommerce-builder/)
- Bangla Landing Page (WP plugin for BD): Bangla Builder + WooCommerce, 126+ Bangla templates, one-click import, landing→checkout→thank-you funnel, incomplete-order recovery, Dhaka inside/outside delivery charge, fake-order protection (phone/IP block, daily order limits), mobile-first Bangla fonts, claimed LCP ~1.1s / page &lt;300KB; priced from ৳99/mo. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/); [FAQ](https://banglalandingpage.com/faq/)
- Eazifiy for WooCommerce: courier fraud check (Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, Paperfly, CarryBee), incomplete-order recovery (call/WhatsApp/SMS), Bangla landing builder (own builder + Elementor + Gutenberg), Meta Pixel+CAPI, TikTok Pixel, GA4, OTP for risky numbers, bKash advance delivery charge; BDT pricing from ৳50/mo promotional / Lifetime ৳999. — [Eazifiy WordPress](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress)
- COD Popup Checkout (Digital Solutions BD): one-click popup COD form (name, phone, address), variation picker, live shipping/coupon, mobile bottom-sheet, Bangla UI—explicitly targeting abandonment vs default Woo checkout. — [COD Popup Checkout](https://digitalsolutionsbd.com/cod-popup-checkout/)
- StackNudge Store Kit: Bangla-first Woo templates for Facebook sellers—guest checkout, Buy Now, COD + bKash/Nagad, WhatsApp ordering, Dhaka delivery rules, mobile-first. — [StackNudge Store Kit](https://stacknudge.digital/products/woocommerce-store-kit-for-facebook-sellers/)
- AAM Partial COD & Mobile Payment: BD-native Woo gateways for partial COD (advance delivery via bKash/Nagad/Rocket), full mobile payment, bank transfer; QR + TrxID; manual admin verification. — [WordPress.org AAM Partial COD](https://wordpress.org/plugins/aam-partial-cod/)
- DigiShop AI (SaaS): theme storefront, COD + bank transfer, WhatsApp Business COD confirmation, Meta/TikTok/Google pixels, funnel/UTM analytics, Google/Meta ads managers, courier booking where integrated—operations + ads, not a WP plugin. — [DigiShop features](https://www.digishop-ai.com/features); [help](https://www.digishop-ai.com/help)

### Inferences
- Table stakes split into two stacks: (A) global “funnel economics” (bumps, one-click upsell, A/B, pixels, step analytics) and (B) BD “COD operations” (3-field mobile order form, Bangla copy/fonts, Dhaka shipping, fake-order controls, incomplete phone capture, bKash/Nagad advance).
- Sellers expect Woo/plugin tools to ride existing payment gateways; SaaS tools expect Stripe/PayPal-class processors—weak fit for BD COD without local adaptation.
- “Professional” packaging repeatedly pairs a free/lite on-ramp with paid unlocks for bumps/upsells/A/B/analytics (CartFlows, FunnelKit) or contact/funnel limits (systeme.io, GetResponse).

### Gaps
- No reliable primary docs found for a WordPress product literally named “DigiShop” as a BD COD landing plugin; DigiShop AI is SaaS and not BD-only.
- Independent, non-affiliate 2024–2026 conversion benchmarks comparing these tools head-to-head for BD traffic were not found.
- Tamai Funnel and many BD theme kits advertise COD one-page checkout, but deep feature matrices beyond marketing pages were thin.

## Which capabilities map to page design freedom, templates, order form UX, payments, post-purchase, analytics/funnel, A/B, pixels, abandoned cart, speed, mobile-first, Bengali/RTL-adjacent UX?

### Takeaway
Capability ownership is layered: design freedom lives in page builders (Elementor/Thrive/Gutenberg); conversion structure lives in funnel plugins; BD trust/speed/COD UX lives in local plugins. Professional roadmaps must decide which layer Noksha owns vs integrates.

### Cited Findings

#### Page design freedom
- Thrive Architect: full-bleed landing pages bypassing theme header/footer; blocks, conditional display, import/export. — [Thrive landing page docs](https://thrivethemes.com/docs/how-to-get-started-with-landing-pages-in-thrive-architect/)
- FunnelKit/CartFlows: design via Elementor, Gutenberg, Divi, Bricks (CartFlows users cite Bricks support); funnel steps are structure, builders are paint. — [FunnelKit WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/funnel-builder/); [CartFlows pricing testimonials](https://cartflows.com/pricing/)
- Elementor: deep visual control of Woo cart/checkout/account widgets without changing Woo field logic. — [Elementor checkout](https://elementor.com/blog/customize-woocommerce-checkout-page/)
- Bangla Landing Page / Eazifiy: own Bangla builders so sellers avoid buying Elementor Pro + CartFlows for BD offer pages. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/); [Eazifiy](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress)

#### Templates
- CartFlows/FunnelKit: premium ready-made funnel/checkout/upsell templates as paid differentiator. — [CartFlows Free vs Pro](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/free-vs-pro/); [FunnelKit WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/funnel-builder/)
- Thrive: 357 landing page templates (gallery claim). — [Thrive gallery](https://thrivethemes.com/landing-pages-gallery/)
- GetResponse: 30+ automated funnel scenarios. — [GetResponse conversion funnel](https://www.getresponse.com/features/conversion-funnel)
- Bangla Landing Page: 126+ category Bangla templates (gadget, fashion, beauty, food, Islamic, courses). — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/)

#### Order form UX
- FunnelKit Pro: one-/two-/three-step and Shopify-style layouts; 14+ custom field types; conditional fields; Google Address Autocomplete; product options/radio deals at checkout. — [FunnelKit WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/funnel-builder/)
- CartFlows: modern one/two-column, multistep/two-step checkout, field editor/reorder, product options, quantity. — [CartFlows Free vs Pro](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/free-vs-pro/)
- BD COD pattern: minimize to name + phone + address (popup or landing), guest checkout, Buy Now, variation picker before order. — [COD Popup Checkout](https://digitalsolutionsbd.com/cod-popup-checkout/); [StackNudge](https://stacknudge.digital/products/woocommerce-store-kit-for-facebook-sellers/)

#### Payment methods
- Global WP funnels: inherit Woo gateways (Stripe, PayPal, COD, etc.). — [CartFlows pricing FAQ](https://cartflows.com/pricing/)
- SaaS: Stripe/PayPal (± Square, NMI, PayU, BlueSnap); ClickFunnels Payments AI adds routing/retries; systeme.io emphasizes 0% platform cut. — [ClickFunnels payments](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/getting-started-setting-up-payments-in-clickfunnels/); [systeme.io pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing); [GetResponse funnel](https://www.getresponse.com/features/conversion-funnel)
- BD: COD primary; bKash/Nagad/Rocket full or partial advance; SSLCOMMERZ in agency builds; manual QR/TrxID verification common. — [AAM Partial COD](https://wordpress.org/plugins/aam-partial-cod/); [EOMSBD WooCommerce BD](https://eomsbd.com/woocommerce-website-bangladesh); [Eazifiy bKash advance](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress)

#### Post-purchase
- Table stakes globally: custom thank-you, order bumps at checkout, one-click upsell/downsell, smart offer routing. — [CartFlows](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/); [FunnelKit](https://funnelkit.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/); [systeme.io funnels](https://systeme.io/sales-funnels/)
- GetResponse: automated order confirmation + product recommendations after purchase. — [GetResponse conversion funnel](https://www.getresponse.com/features/conversion-funnel)
- BD local tools emphasize thank-you + call/WhatsApp confirmation more than card-style one-click upsell (card tokenization often missing for COD). — [DigiShop WhatsApp COD help](https://www.digishop-ai.com/help); [Bangla Landing Page funnel claim](https://banglalandingpage.com/)

#### Analytics / funnel
- CartFlows Pro/Suite and FunnelKit Pro: advanced funnel analytics (transactions, step activity, contacts). — [CartFlows pricing](https://cartflows.com/pricing/); [FunnelKit WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/funnel-builder/)

- DigiShop AI: views, visitors, purchases, revenue, funnel drop-offs, UTM/channel breakdowns, CSV export. — [DigiShop features](https://www.digishop-ai.com/features)

#### A/B testing

#### Pixels (Meta / TikTok / Google)
- FunnelKit docs: Facebook Pixel + Conversion API, GA4, TikTok Pixel; site-wide + funnel events (PageView, Purchase, AddToCart, bumps, checkout); warns against duplicate purchase events with PixelYourSite. — [FunnelKit Facebook CAPI](https://funnelkit.com/docs/funnel-builder/global-settings/facebook-conversion-api/); [GA](https://funnelkit.com/docs/funnel-builder/global-settings/google-analytics/); [TikTok](https://funnelkit.com/docs/funnel-builder/global-settings/tiktok/)

- GetResponse: automatic Facebook Pixel for retargeting in conversion funnel narrative. — [GetResponse blog](https://www.getresponse.com/blog/getresponse-conversion-funnel)

#### Abandoned cart / incomplete order
- CartFlows Suite bundles Cart Abandonment Recovery Pro (email, SMS & WhatsApp). — [CartFlows pricing](https://cartflows.com/pricing/)
- GetResponse: abandoned-order emails as funnel feature; Marketer plan lists abandoned cart recovery. — [GetResponse conversion funnel](https://www.getresponse.com/features/conversion-funnel); [GetResponse pricing plans help](https://www.getresponse.com/help/getresponse-pricing-plans.html)
- BD equivalent is often **incomplete order recovery** (phone typed, order not submitted)—Bangla Landing Page and Eazifiy treat this as core COD loss recovery, not classic email cart abandonment. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/); [Eazifiy](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress)

#### Speed
- Bangla Landing Page markets Facebook-ad speed: LCP 1.1s, CLS 0.01, page size &lt;300KB, score 98—claims 3G/4G friendliness. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/)
- StackNudge cites minimal plugin stack for phone performance. — [StackNudge](https://stacknudge.digital/products/woocommerce-store-kit-for-facebook-sellers/)

#### Mobile-first

#### Bengali / RTL-adjacent UX
- Explicit Bangla UI/templates/support: Bangla Landing Page, Eazifiy, StackNudge, COD Popup Checkout; payment copy editable in any language on AAM Partial COD. — sources above; [AAM Partial COD](https://wordpress.org/plugins/aam-partial-cod/)
- Global tools (ClickFunnels, CartFlows, FunnelKit, Thrive) are English-first in docs/marketing; no strong primary evidence found of first-class Bengali typography or BD address UX in those products.
- True RTL (Arabic/Urdu) was not a documented focus of BD COD tools researched; “RTL-adjacent” for BD is better read as Bangla shaping, mobile density, and phone-first forms than CSS `dir=rtl`.

### Inferences
- Capability map for Noksha: own **order form + COD payments + BD ops + pixels + incomplete recovery**; integrate or light-touch **design freedom**; match global **templates + thank-you + analytics**; treat card-style **one-click upsell** as secondary until prepaid methods grow.
- Abandoned-cart email (SaaS) ≠ incomplete phone capture (BD COD)—roadmaps that only ship email recovery miss the local pattern.

### Gaps
- Pixel event completeness (CAPI, TikTok Events API) for CartFlows vs FunnelKit was only partially verified from CartFlows marketing matrix (lists FB/GA; less TikTok detail on free-vs-pro page).
- Formal RTL support matrices for any competitor were not located.
- Independent speed measurements under BD network conditions were not available.

## What are realistic differentiators for a WordPress plugin aimed at BD COD + bKash/Nagad sellers vs SaaS funnels?

### Takeaway
A WP plugin wins where SaaS loses: host/data ownership, Woo inventory/courier plugins, BDT pricing, Bangla UX, COD fraud workflows, and local wallets—while matching “professional” expectations on pixels, short mobile checkout, and funnel analytics so sellers do not feel stuck on a “local toy.”

### Cited Findings
- SaaS funnels optimize for card/PayPal checkout and contact-based billing (ClickFunnels $97+/mo; systeme.io free→$97; GetResponse Marketer ~$48+/mo annually; GetCourse ~$65+/mo). — [ClickFunnels pricing](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/clickfunnels-pricing/); [systeme.io pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing); [GetResponse trial pricing](https://www.getresponse.com/pricing/trial); [GetCourse help](https://getcourse.io/help)
- CartFlows explicitly says any Woo gateway works and lists COD among one-click upsell–compatible methods—WP stays payment-agnostic. — [CartFlows pricing FAQ](https://cartflows.com/pricing/)
- BD agency/product pages treat bKash merchant, Nagad, SSLCOMMERZ, COD verification calls, Pathao/RedX as the production stack—not Stripe. — [EOMSBD](https://eomsbd.com/woocommerce-website-bangladesh)
- Local plugins differentiate on COD risk: courier history fraud scores, phone/IP blocks, OTP, partial delivery advance via bKash, incomplete-order call lists. — [Eazifiy](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress); [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/); [AAM Partial COD](https://wordpress.org/plugins/aam-partial-cod/)
- Bangla Landing Page’s competitive framing vs Elementor Pro + CartFlows is BDT price, Bangla templates, COD form, fake-order protection, WhatsApp Bangla support—not feature parity on one-click card upsells. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/)
- DigiShop AI shows SaaS can still win on WhatsApp inbox + ads managers + COD filters, but merchants leave WordPress/Woo plugin ecosystem. — [DigiShop features](https://www.digishop-ai.com/features)
- FunnelKit’s pixel stack (Meta CAPI, TikTok, GA4) is the professionalism bar BD Facebook/TikTok advertisers already expect from “serious” tools. — [FunnelKit pixel docs](https://funnelkit.com/docs/funnel-builder/global-settings/facebook-conversion-api/)

### Inferences
- Realistic Noksha differentiators (defensible vs SaaS): (1) COD-native checkout UX in Bangla; (2) bKash/Nagad/Rocket full + partial-advance flows with QR/TrxID or merchant API; (3) incomplete-order + courier fraud ops; (4) Dhaka/outside shipping UX baked into offer pages; (5) WooCommerce ownership (products, HPOS, existing themes); (6) BDT packaging and Bangla support; (7) Meta/TikTok/GA4 without forcing PixelYourSite.
- Weak differentiators if copied blindly from ClickFunnels: courses, communities, native email ESP, Stripe Payments AI—sellers already use Facebook + SMS/WhatsApp.
- Professional signal vs “cheap BD plugin”: ship FunnelKit-grade pixel/CAPI and step analytics *alongside* local COD features—not instead of them.

### Gaps
- No published market-share data for Bangla Landing Page vs Eazifiy vs CartFlows among BD Facebook sellers.
- bKash Checkout API vs manual personal-number flows: adoption rates and seller preference data not found in this pass.
- Legal/compliance constraints for storing COD phone leads (incomplete orders) in BD were not researched.

## Pricing and packaging patterns (free core + paid, SaaS vs plugin) that signal “professional”

### Takeaway
Professional products use a clear free/lite proof path, then paid tiers gated by **sites**, **revenue features** (bumps/upsells/A/B/analytics), or **contacts**—with introductory first-year discounts and 14–30 day money-back. BD peers price in BDT monthly/lifetime and sell via bKash, which itself is a trust pattern for local sellers.

### Cited Findings
- **CartFlows (plugin):** Free on WordPress.org; paid Starter / Pro / Suite. Sale/list examples: Starter $79→renew $149/yr (1 site), Pro $179→renew $299/yr (3 sites), Suite $269→renew $449/yr (30 sites) with abandonment + modern cart + coupons. A/B + advanced analytics sit on higher tiers. 14-day refund. — [CartFlows pricing](https://cartflows.com/pricing/); [Free vs Pro](https://cartflows.com/wordpress-funnel-builder/free-vs-pro/)
- **FunnelKit (plugin suite):** Free Lite + Basic ~$99.50 / Plus ~$179.50 / Professional ~$249.50 / Elite ~$399.50 per year (site counts 1→30); bumps, upsells, A/B, analytics unlock on Plus+; Automations bundled higher. Introductory pricing with renewals at full price. — [FunnelKit Lite vs Pro](https://funnelkit.com/lite-vs-pro/); [FunnelKit FAQ May 2026](https://funnelkit.com/frequently-asked-questions/)
- **Thrive (plugin suite):** Architect ~$99 first year / $199 renew; Architect+Optimize ~$199 / $399; Suite ~$299 / $599 for 5 sites and 8 plugins—A/B sold as Optimize add-on. 30-day money-back. — [Thrive Architect pricing](https://thrivethemes.com/architect/?idev_id=403); [Thrive Suite pricing](https://thrivethemes.com/pricing/)
- **ClickFunnels (SaaS):** All-in-one; tier by workspace/contacts/emails/team—not by removing checkout. $97 / $197 / $297 mo (+ Dominate annual). — [ClickFunnels pricing docs](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/clickfunnels-pricing/)
- **systeme.io (SaaS):** Permanent free plan + Startup $17 / Webinar $47 / Unlimited $97 mo; limits on contacts/funnels/courses; core tools on all plans. — [systeme.io pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing)
- **GetResponse (SaaS):** Starter ~$15.58 / Marketer ~$48.38 / Creator ~$56.58 mo (annual); funnels/ecommerce depth on Marketer+. — [GetResponse pricing trial](https://www.getresponse.com/pricing/trial)
- **GetCourse (SaaS):** Feature-complete plans scaled by active users; Start ~$65/mo or $467/yr. — [GetCourse pricing help](https://getcourse.io/blog/776346)
- **Bangla Landing Page:** ৳99 / ৳199 / ৳1,990 per month with annual discounts up to 46%; bKash/Nagad/Rocket payment; positions against $ Elementor+CartFlows stack. — [Bangla Landing Page](https://banglalandingpage.com/)
- **Eazifiy:** Free 7-day SMS-keyed trial; Starter/Pro monthly in BDT; Lifetime one-time; 30-day bKash refund; pay with bKash. — [Eazifiy](https://eazifiy.com/wordpress)
- **Elementor + Woo:** Pro subscription for checkout widgets; often stacked with a separate funnel plugin—Bangla Landing Page cites Elementor Pro Essential ≈ $60/yr + CartFlows as the expensive combo. — [Elementor ecommerce](https://elementor.com/ecommerce-builder/); [Bangla Landing Page comparison](https://banglalandingpage.com/)

### Inferences
- Patterns that read as professional: (1) free core that ships a real checkout/landing, (2) paid unlocks for optimization (A/B, analytics, dynamic offers), (3) site-count ladders for agencies, (4) visible money-back, (5) transparent renew-at-full-price footnotes, (6) suite bundling (automation/cart/abandonment) at the top SKU.
- For Noksha in BD: BDT monthly + annual + optional lifetime (Eazifiy pattern) + bKash checkout for the plugin itself signals local professionalism; mirroring only USD SaaS pricing would feel foreign.
- Avoid “all features forever free” if aiming premium; use Lite for COD landing + thank-you, Pro for pixels/CAPI depth, fraud/incomplete recovery quotas, A/B, and multi-site.

### Gaps
- Exact current Elementor Pro list prices were not fetched from elementor.com pricing in this pass (Bangla Landing Page’s $60 figure is secondary and FX-dated).
- CartFlows/FunnelKit lifetime options appear in older reviews; official 2026 CartFlows page emphasized annual Starter/Pro/Suite during a sale—lifetime availability should be re-checked at purchase time.
- Competitor churn, NPS, or “professional brand” perception surveys for BD sellers were not found.
